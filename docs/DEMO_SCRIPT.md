# VendorIQ — Demo Script

Suggested walkthrough: 5–7 minutes. Use a clean, isolated database and synthetic data. Do not use confidential supplier or employer information.

## 1. Opening (30 seconds)
“VendorIQ is an AI-assisted sourcing partner. It connects the sourcing brief, supplier discovery, evidence review, RFQ preparation, and quotation analysis in one traceable workflow. It supports buyer decisions; it does not replace buyer approval.”

## 2. Executive Overview (45 seconds)
- Open Executive Overview.
- Explain the active sourcing brief and candidate evaluation summary.
- Point out system/safety status and active database.
- Clarify that candidates are evaluated candidates, not approved suppliers.

## 3. Sourcing Brief (45 seconds)
- Show how the buyer defines the requirement, target industry/geography, and scoring weights.
- Explain that the brief becomes the basis for discovery and evaluation.

## 4. Supplier Discovery and Evaluation (60 seconds)
- Show candidate filtering and shortlisting.
- Explain deterministic scoring dimensions.
- Emphasize that missing data is not proof of poor supplier quality and a score is not certification.

## 5. Evidence & Trust (45 seconds)
- Show VERIFIED, SELF-REPORTED, UNVERIFIED, and MISSING evidence statuses.
- Explain why evidence provenance matters.
- Point out that self-reported claims require independent verification before qualification.

## 6. RFQ Preparation (45 seconds)
- Show RFQ preparation, review/approval, document, and audit workflow.
- Explain that the buyer controls approval and dispatch; VendorIQ does not automatically send supplier messages.

## 7. Quotations & Analysis (90 seconds)
- Show ingestion, review/human confirmation, side-by-side comparison, recommendations, and human approval.
- Use a synthetic PDF/XLSX or manual quotation example.
- Explain extraction provenance, currency-rate disclosure, and incomplete landed-cost handling.
- Demonstrate that a mandatory technical failure cannot be offset by a lower price.
- Emphasize that award is an explicit human action; no purchase order is created.

## 8. Close (20 seconds)
“VendorIQ makes sourcing decisions more structured and explainable by keeping evidence quality, commercial comparison, technical compliance, and human accountability visible throughout the workflow.”

## Demo safety
Use a fresh isolated database and synthetic data only. Do not expose API keys, .env, database files, or uploaded quotation documents. Confirm the active database in the dashboard. Do not claim supplier verification, live FX, automated dispatch, or production deployment unless separately verified.
