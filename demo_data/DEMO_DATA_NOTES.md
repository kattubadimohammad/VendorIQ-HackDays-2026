# Demo Data Notes

Any sample quotation comparison used for the VendorIQ public demo must be explicitly synthetic.

- Supplier names such as Alpha, Beta, and Gamma are fictional placeholders.
- Prices, currencies, technical measurements, delivery terms, and quotation references are illustrative values, not market quotes.
- The sample is not a recommendation of any real supplier.
- Do not combine synthetic records with live supplier records without clearly labeling their provenance.
- Freight, duties, taxes, and other landed-cost components must remain incomplete when the example does not provide them.
- A low price must not compensate for a mandatory technical compliance failure.

Label the UI and demo narration “SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA”.
